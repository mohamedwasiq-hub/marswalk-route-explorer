Yes. We used an AI coding assistant (Claude, a large language model) to help write the code, the documentation and the slide text, working under our direction. We chose the challenge, the study site, the questions to answer and the design, and we reviewed and tested the results. The AI helped write the data processing, the route search, the automated checks and the web interface. No AI-generated images, video or audio are used anywhere: all map imagery is real NASA HiRISE and Viking data, and the demo slides use real screenshots of the app. Every terrain number comes from the NASA HiRISE elevation file, and the 14 validation checks run against the real file. As the rules ask, the AI use is stated in descriptive text and in metadata: in the app's footer, in an "ai-assistance" meta tag in the web page, in a comment at the top of every code file, in the README, and on the last demo slide.
