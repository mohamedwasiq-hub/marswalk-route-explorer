MarsWalk Route Explorer is an interactive web map that helps plan an astronaut's walk across the Jezero Crater delta on Mars, where NASA's Perseverance rover is exploring. It is built on NASA's 1-metre HiRISE elevation model and compares two walking routes: a short direct line, and a terrain-aware path found by a computer search that avoids steep ground. Users can change what matters most (gentle terrain, short distance or data coverage), draw their own route and have it scored the same way, and click any 60-metre stretch to see its slope, its climb and why it scored the way it did. This answers the challenge's call for a layered, hyperlocal map that a human explorer could use to plan a safe Marswalk. Before anyone walks on ground nobody has ever walked on, the trade-offs between routes need to be visible, checkable and honest about their limits. MarsWalk makes that possible for mission planners and for classrooms.
